Week 7 — TRACE Midterm Challenge

An original evidence-based investigation integrating Weeks 1–6

ImportantThis is not a conventional exam

The Week 7 Midterm is an original TRACE investigation.

AI use is permitted.

The assessment is designed so that polished AI-generated prose cannot replace:

  • provenance;
  • your search/interaction trail;
  • your probe decisions;
  • independent evidence;
  • verification;
  • final judgment.

Midterm weight

30% of the course grade

Central challenge

Can you investigate a digital claim, algorithmic system, AI claim, LLM output, or automated decision in a way that another person can reconstruct and audit?

Before class

Case seed

Your candidate case should already have been registered through Week 6.

Review your TRACE evidence

Bring access to your W1–W6 materials.

Complete the readiness quiz

The readiness quiz checks procedures, evidence requirements, and assessment rules.

It is not a content-memory exam.

TRACE Midterm Lab

Session 1 — Authenticate and frame the investigation

Confirm your artifact

Your case may be:

  • a news or social-media claim;
  • a search/recommendation pattern;
  • a claim about algorithmic influence;
  • an AI product/media claim;
  • an LLM output;
  • an automated decision;
  • another instructor-approved digital artifact.

Receive your Challenge Code

Each student receives one additional constraint.

A — Source Origin
Trace the claim toward primary/original evidence.

B — Cross-System Comparison
Test the case in two information or AI systems.

C — Counterfactual Probe
Change one relevant variable at a time and observe the result.

D — Effect vs Exposure
Separate evidence that something was shown/targeted from evidence that it changed behaviour.

E — Capability Scope
Identify what a capability metric establishes and where the claim overgeneralises.

F — Fairness Lens
Apply an appropriate error/fairness criterion to the case.

The instructor may adapt a code when it does not fit the registered case.

Predict

Before new investigation record:

  • initial verdict;
  • initial confidence: 0–100;
  • strongest reason;
  • most important uncertainty.

Frame one investigable question

Weak:

“Is AI dangerous?”

Better:

“Does the evidence supplied for this hiring tool support the claim that it predicts employee performance accurately across the relevant population?”

Session 2 — Probe and evidence trail

Minimum investigation requirements

Your evidence trail must contain:

  • at least three search queries / prompts / interaction variants;
  • at least two meaningful TRACE probes;
  • at least two external sources, where external verification is relevant;
  • at least one rejected result with justification;
  • evidence addressing your Challenge Code.

Evidence classification

For each important item classify it as:

  • Primary/original evidence
  • Independent secondary evidence
  • Claimant/vendor evidence
  • LLM-generated lead
  • Unverified lead
  • Rejected evidence

Explain

At the end of Session 2 write three provisional statements:

  1. What is currently established?
  2. What remains uncertain?
  3. What would be an overclaim?

Session 3 — Decide and verify

Final judgment

Use a verdict appropriate to your case.

Examples:

  • Supported
  • Contradicted
  • Unsupported
  • Missing context
  • Unresolved
  • Mechanism established; effect unknown
  • Capability plausible; evidence incomplete
  • Sensitive to tested variable
  • Evidence insufficient for fairness claim

Recalibrate confidence

Record:

  • Initial confidence: ___ / 100
  • Final confidence: ___ / 100
  • Why confidence changed or did not change

Human judgment statement

Answer:

What part of this investigation could AI assist with but could not legitimately decide for you?

Submit

Submit TRACE Midterm Challenge

Required submission structure

A. Case provenance

  • original artifact;
  • URL/screenshot;
  • where and when you encountered it;
  • Week 6 registration reference.

B. Initial position

  • precise investigable question;
  • initial verdict;
  • initial confidence;
  • strongest initial signal.

C. Search / interaction log

  • three or more queries/prompts/conditions;
  • why each changed;
  • relevant outputs.

D. TRACE probes

  • Probe 1;
  • Probe 2;
  • assigned Challenge Code.

E. Evidence

  • primary/original evidence where available;
  • independent evidence;
  • one rejected result;
  • source relationships.

F. Judgment

  • final verdict;
  • final confidence;
  • inference boundary;
  • strongest remaining uncertainty.

G. AI-use disclosure

  • tool(s);
  • purpose;
  • consequential prompts;
  • what you independently verified;
  • what remained your judgment.

Assessment rubric

Dimension Weight
Case framing and provenance 15%
Search / interaction trail 20%
Quality of probes 20%
Evidence and independent verification 25%
Final judgment and inference discipline 15%
AI-use transparency and process integrity 5%
WarningWhat is not rewarded
  • polished prose without evidence;
  • many links with no source relationship analysis;
  • treating an LLM answer as verification;
  • claiming causation from exposure;
  • claiming general capability from one benchmark;
  • claiming discrimination from one counterfactual example.

After class

Complete any evidence packaging required by the submission deadline announced in the form.

Do not replace your in-class evidence trail with a newly generated narrative.
