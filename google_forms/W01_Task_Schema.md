# DSA261 — Week 01 After-Class Task

## Form title
**DSA261 — Task 01: Source Trace**

## Student-facing description
Choose one verifiable digital claim that you **personally encountered during the last seven days**. Reconstruct how you moved from first impression to a defensible judgment.

AI use is permitted for planning, query generation, translation, or similar support. **AI output is not independent verification evidence.**

## Instructor completion criteria

### COMPLETE
- Authentic original artifact is identifiable.
- Three meaningfully different queries are shown.
- Student distinguishes original/repeated source from independent evidence.
- At least one rejected result is justified.
- Verdict follows from evidence.
- Initial and final confidence are both present.
- AI use, if any, is transparent and is not treated as evidence.

### REVISE
Typical triggers:
- all three queries are superficial variants;
- “independent” evidence merely repeats the same source;
- verdict is stronger than available evidence;
- missing provenance/search trail;
- AI answer used as factual verification.

### MISSING
- No authentic claim/artifact;
- no traceable search process;
- only opinion or generated prose submitted;
- evidence links absent without explanation.
